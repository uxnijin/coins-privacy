Coins Privacy Policy

Coins: Expense Tracker · Last updated 31 October 2026

Coins is a personal expense tracker. It is built so that your money data stays with you. We don't run servers for Coins, we don't have accounts, and we can't see what you enter.

What the app stores

Everything you add (accounts, categories, expenses and income, budgets, subscriptions, splits, inbox messages you paste, and your profiles) is stored on your device only.

iCloud sync

If you turn on iCloud sync, your data for the main profile is copied to your private iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit, so it can sync across your devices. This data sits in your own iCloud storage, governed by Apple's privacy policy. We have no access to it. Turning sync off stops future syncing.

What we collect

Nothing. Coins has no analytics, advertising, tracking or third-party SDKs, and it does not connect to any server of ours. We don't sell, share or receive your data.

Sharing with AI and other apps

When you use “Share with AI” or export, Coins builds a summary or file on your device and opens the iOS share sheet. Nothing leaves your phone until you pick an app. What happens after that is governed by the app you choose (for example ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini) and its own privacy policy. The shared file contains no card or bank account numbers.

Share extension and pasted messages

Links you share to Coins (for example a subscription page) and bank messages you paste into the inbox are read on your device to fill in an entry. They are not sent anywhere.

Notifications

Reminders, such as an upcoming subscription renewal, are scheduled locally on your device. Coins does not use push notifications from a server.

Deleting your data

Deleting a profile in Settings removes its data from the device. Deleting the app removes all local data. If you used iCloud sync, you can remove the synced data in iOS Settings › your name › iCloud › Manage Storage.

Children

Coins is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
